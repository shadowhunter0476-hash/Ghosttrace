Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use GhostTrace, we collect:

  • Account data: email, hashed password
  • Usage data: search queries, timestamps, credits used
  • Technical data: IP address, browser, device

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the Service
  • To manage your account and credits
  • To prevent abuse and enforce our Terms
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. What We Do NOT Do

  • We do not sell your data to third parties
  • We do not access your private accounts or messages
  • We do not use your data for advertising
  • We do not share data except when legally required

4. Search Queries

Your search queries (username, email, or phone) are logged in your account history for your convenience. You can clear this history at any time from the dashboard.

5. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. Passwords are hashed using industry-standard methods. We retain data as long as your account is active.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Delete your account and all data
  • Export your search history
  • Opt out of non-essential data collection

To exercise these rights, use the Settings page or contact us.

7. Cookies

We use a single session cookie to keep you logged in. No tracking cookies, no third-party analytics.

8. Children's Privacy

GhostTrace is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be notified via email or dashboard notice.
